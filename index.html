05 / FAQ

Good questions, clear answers.

A few things worth knowing before we start.

How much does a website cost?

My website projects generally range from $500 to $1,800 USD, depending on the number of pages, design, and features. I currently focus on websites with up to five pages. You can try my pricing calculator for an estimate; final pricing is discussed before a project begins.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment methods, any deposit, and the payment schedule are discussed and agreed upon before work starts. I'll make sure the terms are clear for your specific project.

Can you add an AI chatbot to my website?

Yes, we can discuss a chatbot that answers common questions or helps visitors find information. I'll first learn what your business needs, then explain what is practical for your website and budget.
